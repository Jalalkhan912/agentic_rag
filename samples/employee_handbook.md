# Northwind Labs — Employee Handbook (2026)

## 1. Working Hours
Core hours are 10:00–16:00 local time. Outside core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as they work 40 hours per week.

## 2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head. A one-time home-office stipend of $600 is available in the first 90 days of employment.

## 3. Paid Time Off
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid vacation per year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over to the next calendar year; any remaining days expire on March 31.
Sick leave is unlimited but absences longer than 3 consecutive days require a doctor's note.

## 4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken any time within the first year.

## 5. Learning Budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and conferences. Conference travel is covered separately when the employee is presenting.

## 6. Expenses
Expenses must be submitted within 30 days with a receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Business-class flights are only permitted for flights longer than 8 hours.
